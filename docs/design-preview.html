Signal — design system for Sage Vault

A foundation rebuild: tokens, type scale, spacing, and the seven primitives in src/components/ui. Every one of the 147 pages inherits from these. Nothing here is page-specific yet — that comes after you approve the base.

01 — What changes and why

The core problem: emerald means three different things at once. Today emerald-500 is the brand colour, the success colour, and the "progress ≥ 70%" colour. On a security platform that is a real defect — a user can't tell whether green means "this is a button", "this passed", or "this is our brand". Signal splits them: azure is interactive, emerald is exclusively success/secure.
Retired effects. .scanline, .noise, .text-glow, .glow-pulse, .orb-drift, .card-hover lift, and the bg-grid overlay come off all data surfaces. They reduce legibility on dense screens and read as decoration rather than meaning. A restrained subset stays for marketing pages only.
Kept from the generator. Product type Cybersecurity Platform → dashboard style Real-Time Monitoring + Data-Dense: 8px grid gap, 12px card padding, 36px table rows, 240px sidebar, 56px header, and the Fira Code / Fira Sans pairing. The generator also proposed Matrix green #00FF41 on pure black — declined, that is the cliché the app already has, and pure black plus neon fails contrast comfort on long sessions.
Measured against the actual codebase. emerald-* appears 725 times across 230 files — the overload is systemic, which is exactly why it can't be fixed page by page. The decorative effects being retired are far cheaper: 39 occurrences across 15 files, concentrated in app/page.tsx (16) and complete-profile (4). One real defect found while auditing: text-zinc-600 on #09090b — used for StatCard sub-labels — measures 2.57:1 and fails even the 3:1 large-text floor. Signal's --text-3 replacement measures 4.10:1.

04 — Status & severity

Status answers "what happened". Severity answers "how bad". They are separate ramps so a resolved critical incident can render both truthfully. Every severity carries a glyph as well as a hue — colour is never the only signal.
